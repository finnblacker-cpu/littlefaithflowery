Fresh, handmade bouquets — made with care

Little Faith Flowery is a small, home-based floral studio in the East Bay. Every bouquet is cut and arranged by hand in small batches, using seasonal blooms sourced locally. What started as a hobby has grown into custom bouquets for birthdays, just-because days, and everything in between.

Follow along on Instagram for the latest arrangements, or reach out to order your own.
